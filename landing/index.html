FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Capibo?

Capibo is a platform for collecting real-world video to train AI models. You describe what your model should learn, Capibo turns it into a data-collection mission, and people around the world record the tasks on their phones. Accepted clips land in your datasets, ready for training.

How do I create a mission?

Describe your goal in plain language, for example “I want a robot that folds clothes”. The Capibo planner breaks it into recordable tasks (pick a garment, lay it flat, fold a t-shirt…) with clip lengths and video counts. You can switch tasks off, change the counts or add your own before approving the plan.

How much does a mission cost?

Before anything launches you get a cost estimate: a price per accepted video that scales with clip length, plus a small QA processing fee per submission. Missions are paid from prepaid credits, and you only pay the video price for clips that pass review.

Who records the videos?

Collectors using the Capibo mobile app. When you launch a mission it is pushed to collectors matched by region, device and rating, with recording guidelines translated into their language.

How do you check quality?

Every submission goes through automated QA: hands in frame, lighting, motion blur, task completion and privacy. Clips that fail are rejected with a reason, and each mission shows acceptance rates per task so you can see which steps need attention.

How is privacy handled?

Guidelines ask for chest- or head-mounted footage with no faces in frame, and the privacy check flags faces or screens before a clip is accepted. Collectors consent to every recording, and you get clear rights to use the footage for training.

What formats can I export?

Accepted videos export with their labels and quality metadata as LeRobot (Hugging Face), RLDS / TFRecord, WebDataset shards or MP4 + JSON. Send them to Amazon S3, a private Hugging Face organization or a signed download link, or automate it with API keys and webhooks.

How quickly will I get data?

Collection starts as soon as the mission is dispatched, and the estimate shows the expected delivery time for the whole mission. Accepted videos appear in your datasets as they pass QA, so you can start training on partial data and pause or resume the mission at any time.
