Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy explains what personal data Capibo collects, why, and what you can do about it. It covers this website and the platform at app.capibo.work.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password (stored only in hashed form).
  • Mission details: the tasks, requirements and settings you enter when you create a mission.
  • Billing details: invoices and payment records. Card details are handled by our payment provider and never stored by us.
  • Usage data: basic technical information such as browser type, pages visited and errors, so we can keep the platform working.
  • Contributor videos: videos recorded for missions may show contributors and their surroundings. Contributors agree to this before recording, and we review clips to remove personal details that aren't needed.

2. How we use it

We use your data to run your account, deliver missions and datasets, bill you, provide support, keep the platform secure, and tell you about important changes. We do not sell your personal data, and we don't use it for advertising.

3. Who we share it with

We share data only with the service providers that help us run Capibo (for example hosting, databases, email and payments), and only as much as they need. We may also share data when the law requires it. Delivered datasets are shared with the customer who ordered them.

4. Cookies

We use only the cookies and local storage needed to keep you signed in and remember your settings, such as light or dark mode. We don't use advertising cookies.

5. How long we keep it

We keep account data while your account is open. When you close it, we delete or anonymise your personal data within 90 days, except records we must keep by law, such as invoices.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct, export or delete your personal data, or object to how we use it. Email team@timefactories.com and we'll reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit, hashed passwords and limited internal access. No system is perfectly secure, but we'll tell you promptly if a breach affects your data.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. If the changes are significant, we'll tell you by email or in the platform.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email team@timefactories.com.
